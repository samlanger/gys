Place Transformation Atlas

Towns and cities reinvent themselves all the time, with a museum, a festival, a film, a wine trail or a cap on visitors. This tool shows you places that started out like yours, what they tried, and how it turned out, including who benefited and who lost out.

  1. Describe your townUse the form on the left. It starts with a made-up example town that you can edit or clear.
  2. Explore the resultsThe tabs on the right update as you click: similar places, ideas that fit, and questions for your board.
  3. Take it to a meetingThe Board briefing tab turns it all into a one-page summary you can copy.
